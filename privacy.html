Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

The short version

  • We do not store your apple images. Photos are processed only to produce the assessment result and are discarded once the result is returned.
  • We do not collect personal data for profiling, advertising or resale. We never sell or rent information.
  • Everything is encrypted. Data moves between your device and our systems over encrypted connections (TLS 1.2+), end to end.
  • You stay in control. Ask us any time what we hold about you, or to delete it: info@maluslens.com.

1. Who we are

Malus Lens (“Malus Lens”, “we”, “us”) provides an AI-powered apple quality assessment platform for businesses involved in apple intake, storage and handling, including cold-storage operations in Kashmir. This policy explains how we handle information on our website (maluslens.com and malus-lens.vercel.app) and in the Malus Lens application.

2. Images you capture

When your team photographs a sample of apples in the Malus Lens application, the images are sent over an encrypted connection to be analysed by our computer-vision models. The analysis produces quality information such as apple counts, grade percentages and estimated weights.

  • Images are processed in memory for the purpose of that assessment only.
  • Images are not saved to our servers, not kept in a library and not reused after the result is returned.
  • Images are not used to train our models unless your organisation has agreed to it separately in writing.
  • Images are not shared with third parties.

Apple images are pictures of produce, not of people. Please avoid capturing people in frame; if a person appears by accident, the image is still discarded after processing.

3. Assessment results

The numerical results of an assessment (for example: lot reference, number of apples detected, grade percentages, estimated weight and the date) belong to your organisation. They are kept so your team can view history and download reports, and they are visible only to authorised users of your organisation’s account. Your organisation can ask us to delete them at any time.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect personal data beyond what is strictly needed to provide the service. We do not:

  • sell, rent or trade any information;
  • use advertising trackers or build marketing profiles;
  • collect location, contacts, or other device data from the application;
  • collect sensitive personal data.

5. The limited information we do handle

  • Account details needed to sign in to the application (name, work email or mobile number, and a securely hashed password), managed by your organisation.
  • Meeting requests. If you book a meeting on our website, the name, email, organisation and note you type are used only to create the Google Calendar invitation and Google Meet link for that meeting. The website itself does not store them; the invitation lives in the Malus Lens calendar.
  • Messages you send us by email or phone, used only to reply to you.
  • Basic technical logs (such as error logs kept by our hosting provider for a short period) needed to keep the service secure and working. They are not used to identify or profile visitors.

6. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted end to end in transit using HTTPS/TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Stored results and account data are encrypted at rest by our infrastructure providers.
  • Access is restricted to authorised users with role-based permissions; passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • We apply security updates promptly and review access regularly.

No system is perfectly secure, but if we ever become aware of a breach affecting your information we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

7. Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the service, such as cloud hosting and Google Workspace (for calendar invitations and email). They process information only on our instructions and only to provide their service to us.

8. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It may use strictly necessary browser storage to make the page work (for example, remembering that a dialog was closed).

9. Retention

Images: not retained. Assessment results and account details: kept while your organisation uses Malus Lens and deleted on request or within a reasonable period after the account ends. Meeting requests: kept in our calendar only as long as needed to hold and follow up on the meeting.

10. Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate another person to exercise these rights. Email info@maluslens.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

11. Grievance officer

For any concern about how your information is handled, contact our Grievance Officer at info@maluslens.com or +91 78899 24078. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 days.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be communicated to customers directly.
